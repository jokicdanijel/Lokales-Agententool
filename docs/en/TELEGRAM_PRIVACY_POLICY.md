# Standard Privacy Policy for Bots and Mini‑Apps (Telegram)

**Last updated:** 2025-12-16

This policy applies to third‑party bots and mini‑apps provided on the Telegram platform. It explains what data a third‑party service collects, how it is processed, and your rights as a user.

## 1. Terms and definitions

- **Telegram:** Telegram Messenger Inc.
- **Platform:** The Telegram Bot platform.
- **Developer:** The person or organization operating the third‑party service.
- **Third‑party service:** The bot or mini‑app offered by the developer.
- **User:** A person using the third‑party service.

## 2. General provisions

- This is a standard policy; developers may publish their own, more specific policies.
- The policy governs the relationship between the user and the third‑party service; it does not replace Telegram's privacy policy.
- By using the service you agree to this policy.

## 3. Disclaimers

- Third‑party services are independent from Telegram and maintained by their developers.
- Developers may update the policy and are responsible for notifying users in accordance with applicable law.

## 4. Personal data collection

- Data may be collected when users send messages, upload files, or provide contact details.
- Mini‑apps may collect additional data as described in their documentation.
- Anonymous telemetry and usage statistics may be collected.

## 5. Processing personal data

- Only data necessary for the service operation are processed.
- Processing is performed to provide and improve the service and to detect security incidents.
- Data is not used outside the intended purpose without explicit consent.

## 6. Data protection

- Developers implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data.
- Data sharing with third parties only occurs with consent or when legally required.

## 7. Rights and obligations

- Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data.
- Developers must respond to lawful requests within applicable timeframes (typically 30 days).

## 8. Changes to the policy

- Changes take effect upon publication; users should review updates regularly.

---

_Note:_ This standard policy is generic. Developers must ensure compliance with local laws (e.g., GDPR) and add additional provisions when necessary.
